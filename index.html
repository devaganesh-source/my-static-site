State of West Virginia Office of Inspector General

Fraud Case Management System (RFP)

3.0 Project Scope and Core Requirements

The successful Vendor must provide a Commercial-Off-The-Shelf (COTS) or custom software solution that meets the following operational and technical requirements:

  • Establishing a centralized system of record for fraud-related complaints, investigations, evidence, and outcomes.
  • Evidence and document management with chain-of-custody and audit trails.
  • Role-based access control and immutable logging.
  • Protecting criminal justice information, personally identifiable information, and protected health information throughout the data lifecycle.
  • Providing supervisors and administrators with visibility into case status, workload, timelines, and outcomes.

4.0 Submission and Evaluation

Vendors must respond to each requirement explicitly. Failure to meet the security and audit requirements will result in immediate disqualification.
